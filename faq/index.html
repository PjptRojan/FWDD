Membership Questions

Last updated: October 2023
Joining is simple! You can register through our online portal by clicking the "Membership" link in the navigation bar. You'll need to provide your university email and student ID. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, and you'll typically hear back within 3-5 business days.
Standard membership is free for all current university students. Some specialized professional tracks or workshops may have a nominal fee to cover materials, but these are always clearly communicated in advance.
You can access our Peer Tutoring directory under the Resources tab. Once logged in as a member, you can book sessions directly with our certified student tutors who have excelled in specific coursework modules.
Elections are held annually at the end of the Spring semester. Active members with at least one year of tenure are eligible to run for positions. The voting process is transparent and conducted digitally through our student portal.
Yes! We have a dedicated Graduate Chapter that focuses on advanced research, networking, and professional development suitable for Master's and PhD candidates.
Members receive access to exclusive study materials, priority registration for workshops, networking opportunities with alumni, and a supportive community of peers dedicated to academic excellence.
